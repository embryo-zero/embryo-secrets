# Embryo Secrets

Embryo Secrets is Embryo-Zero's fork of [openbao](https://github.com/openbao/openbao) (5f03f3f, commit 5f03f3f). It does keeps passwords, keys and certificates.

- Our changes live on the `embryo` branch. The original license files (LICENSE) are unchanged.
- Legal notes: LEGAL.md. Dated list of our changes: CHANGES-EMBRYO.md.
